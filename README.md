# Descriptive Analytics

This repository contains descriptive analytics projects across multiple domains, including workforce analytics, healthcare, human resources, public health, and international development. Projects explore data-driven approaches through visualization, statistical analysis, data validation, and evidence-based reporting.

## Analytical Projects Portfolio

| Project | Analytical Focus | Domain Context | Key Analytical Contributions |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AI Impact on Jobs 2030](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Descriptive-Analytics/tree/main/AI%20Impact%20on%20Jobs%202030) | Occupational automation risk assessment and workforce transformation analysis | Labor market and workforce analytics | Descriptive analysis of automation vulnerability, occupational profiling, statistical testing, and mixed-effects modeling to identify structural drivers of AI-related job risk |
| [Healthcare Data Quality and Clinical Validation Pipeline](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Descriptive-Analytics/tree/main/EDA%20in%20a%20Global%20Health%20Context) | Healthcare data reliability and analytical dataset preparation | Healthcare data management | Data quality assessment, clinical rule validation, longitudinal consistency checks, and transformation of raw medical records into a reliable analytical dataset |
| [Gender Pay Gap Analysis](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Descriptive-Analytics/tree/main/Gender%20Pay%20Gap) | Compensation equity assessment through workforce structure analysis | Human resources and organizational analytics | Workforce composition analysis, hypothesis testing, adjusted compensation modeling, and evaluation of occupational segregation effects |
| [Global Conflict Analysis](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Descriptive-Analytics/tree/main/Global%20Conflict%20Analysis) | Conflict dynamics through temporal, geographic, and statistical analysis | International affairs and humanitarian analytics | Exploration of conflict evolution, severity patterns, geographic concentration, and relationship between conflict intensity and media visibility |
| [Global Health and Life Expectancy Analysis](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Descriptive-Analytics/tree/main/Life%20Expectancy%20(WHO)) | Structural assessment of global health disparities | Public health and socioeconomic analytics | Composite health index construction using PCA, regional disparity analysis, statistical testing, and longitudinal assessment of health inequalities |

## Organization
Each project is contained in its own folder at the root of this repository.

## Analytical Toolkit

Statistical analysis | Data visualization | Data quality assessment | Hypothesis testing | Regression modeling | Dashboard development | Exploratory analysis

## Notes on Project Maturity
This repository serves both as a portfolio and as a continuous development environment. Some projects are intentionally scoped to explore specific analytical techniques, visualization approaches, or structural patterns. Insights and improvements from these projects are progressively consolidated into a shared analytical framework used across more comprehensive end-to-end projects.
